# Crypto Learning Curriculum

Daily lessons, one topic per day, in order. The Daily Crypto Lesson agent picks the next topic after the most recent lesson file (by date in filename) in this folder, excluding INDEX.md. Lessons are saved here as YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.md and listed in INDEX.md.

## Curriculum (in order)

1. Candlestick anatomy: body, wicks, open and close meaning
2. Reversal candlesticks: doji, hammer, shooting star, pin bar
3. Continuation candles: engulfing, harami, three white soldiers
4. Support and resistance: how levels form, role flipping
5. Trendlines and channels: drawing rules, breakouts
6. Market structure: HH/HL uptrend, LL/LH downtrend, structure breaks
7. Moving averages: SMA vs EMA, golden/death cross, MA stack
8. RSI: overbought/oversold, divergence, trend-filtered RSI
9. MACD: crossovers, histogram, divergence
10. Bollinger Bands: squeeze, mean reversion, band walking
11. ATR and volatility: stop placement, position sizing
12. Volume analysis: OBV, volume profile, VWAP, volume confirmation
13. Chart patterns 1: head and shoulders, double top/bottom
14. Chart patterns 2: triangles, flags, wedges, pennants
15. Fibonacci retracement and extension: how to draw, confluences
16. Multi-timeframe analysis: HTF bias, LTF entry
17. Ichimoku Cloud: components and basic signals
18. Stochastic and other oscillators (Williams %R, CCI)
19. Order flow basics: bid/ask, tape reading, CVD, footprint
20. Derivatives: funding rates, open interest, long/short ratio
21. Liquidations: cascade mechanics, liquidation maps
22. On-chain analytics: MVRV, SOPR, exchange flows, whale moves
23. Market cap, dominance and altcoin rotation (BTC.D, ETH/BTC)
24. Stablecoin supply and exchange reserves (liquidity cycles)
25. Macro: FOMC, CPI, DXY, interest rates vs crypto
26. Bitcoin halving cycles and seasonality
27. Wyckoff method: accumulation, markup, distribution, markdown
28. Elliott Wave basics and how not to overuse it
29. Risk management: R:R ratio, stop placement, max risk per trade
30. Trade journaling and backtesting basics

After the last topic, loop back to 1.
