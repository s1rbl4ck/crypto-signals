## Candlestick anatomy

Every candle is a snapshot of a fight between buyers and sellers in one time period. Reading bodies and wicks tells you who won and how convincingly.

## Key Concepts
- **Body**: distance between open and close. Green body = close above open (buyers won), red body = sellers won. Big body = conviction, small body = hesitation.
- **Upper wick** (shadow): price went up but got rejected, sellers pushed it back down. Long upper wick near resistance = selling pressure.
- **Lower wick**: price fell then got bought back up. Long lower wick near support = buying pressure.
- **Range**: high minus low = volatility of that period, directly related to ATR.
- **Close matters most**: the close is the period's agreed price, more significant than open or wicks for the next candle.

## Reading it on a chart
- A candle that closes in the top third of its range = buyers in control, momentum up.
- A candle that closes in the bottom third = sellers in control.
- A series of candles with shrinking bodies and long wicks = indecision, volatility compression, often precedes a move (see Bollinger squeeze later in the curriculum).
- Large body with tiny wicks = one-sided move, strong trend day.

## Applying it to signals
- Interpret the daily TA lines: RSI and MACD tell you momentum, but candles tell you WHERE the fight happens. A long lower wick at the script's support level ($62,510 for BTC on 1d) confirms buyers defended it, giving a higher-quality buy-the-dip than a clean rejection close.
- Use candle closes to confirm levels from the S/R computations: a support level only matters if candles show wicks or closes holding above it.
- Avoid entering mid-range: wait for a close near a level with a clear body in the intended direction.

## Common Pitfalls
- Judging a candle by its wick alone, a long wick with a tiny body still shows the level held, but the close near the middle means the next candle decides.
- Ignoring timeframe: a long lower wick on 4h is a different signal than the same shape on 1d. Higher timeframe wicks matter more.
- Assuming a single candle is a pattern: single candles are clues, not signals. Confirmation is the next candle or a volume spike.
