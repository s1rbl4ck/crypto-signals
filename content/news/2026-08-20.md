## Lessons from today's news
- The data pipeline itself is a single point of failure: search and extract share one backend, so when it goes down the whole analysis is blocked at once. A resilient loop needs a fallback source or at least graceful degradation.
- It is far better to record "no data" than to fabricate or guess news. Injected or invented headlines corrupt the learned signal and can drive wrong trades. Honest absence beats plausible fiction.
- Monitoring/tool-health should be part of the loop's output so outages are surfaced at the destination instead of silently producing an empty or fake note.
