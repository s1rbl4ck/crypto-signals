- **Market impact (BTC/ETH/altcoins):** No new SEC dates until the Sept 15 vote, so rulemaking is parked for a month. The legislative overhang keeps an institutional "regulatory discount" in place and is a cap on upside; any Sept 15 headlines (or odds movement) become the next price catalyst. Delays were treated as noise — BTC barely moved on the cancellations.
