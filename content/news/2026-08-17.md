## Lessons from today's news

- **Probability revisions are the trade; the bill text isn't.** Galaxy's 75→60→50→10% de-rating moved regulatory sentiment at every step, while the SEC's actual meeting cancellation moved nothing — markets price odds and deadlines, not process. Track research-projected odds (Galaxy, Polymarket) as the leading indicator for regulatory-driven moves.
- **Stale data still moves headlines: read it for direction, discount it for timing.** The "UBS 24x calls" story is June 30 data re-narrated in mid-August, yet it shapes sentiment — and every dramatic 13F stat carries caveats (no strikes, hedging vs. proprietary) that the headline drops. Directional confirmation is real; don't trade the timestamp.
- **Security breaches don't move prices; the scam wave they seed does.** "Funds safe" breaches (SafePal, Bits of Gold) had no price impact, but each one replenishes the phishing supply chain (MiCA migration scams, fake apps) — the market risk is second-order: impersonation-driven withdrawals and sudden small-cap dumps, plus reputational drag on wallet tokens.
- **Back-to-back scheduled catalysts = gap risk, not trend risk.** Atkins and Garlinghouse on Tuesday, the White House meeting Wednesday, cloture Sept 15 — when the calendar stacks events, expect headline-driven whipsaws and thin weekend liquidity to exaggerate moves; size for events, not levels.
